# GhanaNice design story

The visit begins at a national threshold: the Independence Arch under incoming weather. The page then rejects the idea of Ghana as one image and opens into five photographic dispatches, each with a real coordinate and region. The field atlas turns orientation into interaction. A working seamstress brings the story from monument to maker. The AI demonstration finishes the arc by showing technology as an editor of local signals—not the author of Ghanaian identity.

The reference to LXL Creative is conceptual: editorial collision, film-strip presentation, scale shifts, and scroll-led reveals. GhanaNice keeps its own subject matter, typography, palette, and interaction grammar.

## Landing-page threshold

The Ghana silhouette is present from the first frame on a quiet paper field. Eight fast Ghanaian signals—coast, waterfall, history, work, architecture, canopy and weather—play only inside that silhouette before settling on Independence Arch. The final frame becomes a real looping film: the landmark stays locked while the sky moves. Only then does scroll enlarge the map beyond the viewport and reveal “Ghana, in full.”

The next act is a field of sixteen perforated region postcards. Scroll pulls the cards horizontally through view and lets them resolve into an ordered contact sheet. Choosing one makes the actual cards assemble into a visible deck at the left, with the chosen card on top. A compact regional page opens beside the deck, so place, image and caption remain part of one continuous spatial scene.

The closing act is evidence rather than brand copy: a scroll-driven visitor-flow sculpture ranks Ghana’s ten most visited attractions in the 2024 Ghana Tourism Authority report. Ten streams arrive from alternating directions, settle into a measured field and reveal their proportional footfall. The data gives the page a useful national snapshot instead of another generic manifesto or call-to-action block.
