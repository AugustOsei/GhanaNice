# GhanaNice design story

The visit begins at a national threshold: the Independence Arch under incoming weather. The page then rejects the idea of Ghana as one image and opens into five photographic dispatches, each with a real coordinate and region. The field atlas turns orientation into interaction. A working seamstress brings the story from monument to maker. The AI demonstration finishes the arc by showing technology as an editor of local signals—not the author of Ghanaian identity.

The reference to LXL Creative is conceptual: editorial collision, film-strip presentation, scale shifts, and scroll-led reveals. GhanaNice keeps its own subject matter, typography, palette, and interaction grammar.
