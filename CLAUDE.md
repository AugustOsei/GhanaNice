# GhanaNice

A people-powered guide to good places across Ghana's 16 regions, "for locals and visitors alike". Owner: Augustine Osei (credited in the footer; writes The August Dispatch).

## Stack and layout
- Plain static site: HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. No framework. **Everything deployed lives in `dist/`.** The only generated pages are committed: the region pages and sitemap (`node tools/build_regions.mjs`), the About/Contact/Privacy pages (`node tools/build_pages.mjs`), the festival pages (`node tools/build_festivals.mjs`), the December page (`node tools/build_december.mjs`) and `credits.html`.
- Live at **https://www.ghananice.com** (apex `ghananice.com` 308-redirects to www). Deployed on Vercel from `main` (auto-deploys on push). `vercel.json` sets `outputDirectory: "dist"`. Without it Vercel served the repo root and returned 404.
- Local preview: `.claude/launch.json` → `python3 -m http.server 4173 --directory dist`.
- Cache-busting is manual: bump `?v=N` on the `<link>` / `<script>` tags (`config.js` and `newsletter.js` too) in `index.html`, `tools/region-template.html`, `tools/page-template.html`, the two festival templates and `tools/december-template.html` (then rebuild those pages) and `travel-directory.html` (and `credits.html`/`404.html` for CSS) when changing CSS or JS, and the matching pins in `tools/smoke.mjs`, which Vercel runs as the build. `vercel.json` caches CSS/JS for a day and `/assets/` for 30 days, so give a changed photo a new filename rather than overwriting it.
- Photos in `assets/real/` have `-400/-800/-1200.webp` copies made by `tools/media/resize_local.py` (cards, tiles, heroes). Pages use the copies; the lightbox and CREDITS.md use the originals. Re-run it after adding a photo there.
- `dist/credits.html` is generated from `assets/CREDITS.md` by `tools/media/build_credits.py`. Re-run after editing the credits.

## Rules
- Imagery must show real named places in the region it's filed under. Credit every photo (author, licence, source). Generated media may change atmosphere but must not invent landmarks.
- Copy: plain, warm, accurate. Don't claim content the site doesn't have (we removed "workshops" and "there is room").
- The cards look like postcards but the copy calls them regions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep keyboard and screen-reader paths working.

## Planned features (agreed 2026-10-14, extended 2026-10-24; after the open items above are cleared)
1. **Travel directory**: done (`travel-directory.html`).
2. **History of Ghana**: an immersive, scroll-led story page. Not photos or photorealism. The look is illustrated and layered, like paper-cut scenes (parallax layers of cut-paper shapes and textures) that move, assemble and change as the reader scrolls, with short captions telling the story. Rough eras: early peoples and states (Bono, Dagbon, Mamprugu), Asante, coast trade and the forts/castles and the slave trade, colonial Gold Coast, the road to independence (6 March 1957), the republic today. Illustrations may be stylised but must stay historically accurate: no invented events, landmarks or symbols. Cite sources. Needs a reduced-motion version (static scenes plus text) and must read fully with a screen reader.
3. **Cultural calendar**: national holidays and key festivals and events through the year, month by month, each linked to its region (e.g. Homowo, Aboakyere, Hogbetsotso, Damba, Kundum, Chale Wote, PANAFEST / Emancipation Day). Mark dates that move each year (lunar, traditional-council set) as approximate and say who sets them. Festivals part done 2026-10-16 (`/festivals/`, on the homepage and every region page); national holidays still to add.

**Return visits** (agreed 2026-10-16): the site looks good but a returning visitor sees the same thing, so the aim is real, changing content, not more motion or shuffled content.
4. **Monthly newsletter** (pitched on the site since 2026-10-24 as "Follow GhanaNice as it grows"; sign-ups currently broken, see open item 1): sign-ups built 2026-10-16 on MailerLite (free to ~1,000 subscribers; key `MAILERLITE_API_KEY` in `.env.local`): forms on every page, n8n workflow, double opt-in, privacy section (`docs/newsletter-pipeline.md`). Monthly issue built 2026-10-17: n8n drafts it on the 24th (MailerLite draft + paste-ready email, since the free plan can't take the body via API), August sends it, then one click publishes it at `/newsletter/<yyyy-mm>/`. Drafts arrive monthly from 24 Oct 2026; August starts sending once there are enough subscribers (the November 2026 draft was not sent). The strongest reason to come back, because it brings people back. Content mostly exists: that month's festivals, newly added places and festivals, a few approved community tips. Could be a section of The August Dispatch or a sister letter; decide the email service first (probably whatever the Dispatch uses). Needs a sign-up form (and a line on `/privacy/` for what it collects) and a homepage/footer sign-up.
5. **"New on GhanaNice" on the homepage**: a small strip generated at build time from what was recently added (festivals, places, directory listings), plus newest approved tips first with dates. Cheap, honest freshness: every addition visibly changes the homepage.
6. **Blog ("Stories" or "Guides"), agreed as a major feature 2026-10-24**: guides people search for (e.g. "A weekend in Cape Coast", "Getting to Mole", "What to eat at Homowo"), each linking into region, festival and directory pages; the festival pages are the model (generated from data, sourced, JSON-LD). Build it only once there is a realistic publishing cadence (who writes, how often; two good posts a month beats an empty blog).
7. **Experiences** (agreed 2026-10-24): a page of things people can book, such as tours, classes, tastings and day trips, each as a package with what's included, dates or availability, price and who runs it. The ticket-style card tried for the December featured events felt out of place there but suits bookings: its code and styles are kept in `docs/experiences-ticket.md` as the starting point. Needs a way to book or enquire (decide: enquiry to the provider, or real booking and payment), and item 8 for providers.
8. **Admin for providers** (agreed 2026-10-24): a place where travel businesses can list themselves or create and edit experience packages, instead of everything going through email and the n8n approval flow. Needs sign-in, approval before anything goes live (keep the review step), and a decision on where the data lives (the site is static: today listings are JSON committed by n8n). Builds on `/list-your-service/` and `docs/service-directory-pipeline.md`.
9. **Better analytics** (agreed 2026-10-24): see which pages get the most visits, how long people stay and where they go next, plus the site's own actions (December plan saves and shares, filter use, tip and newsletter sign-ups, outbound clicks). Google Analytics (G-07N1MXC0Q9) already loads on every page through `analytics.js` and honours the opt-out on `/privacy/`; start by sending named events from those actions and setting up GA4 reports, then decide whether a simpler dashboard is worth it. Update `/privacy/` for anything new that is measured.
