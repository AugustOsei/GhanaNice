Got photos nicer than nice?

Some regions still have few pictures, Ahafo and Western North most of all, and some places have none. If you’ve taken good photos of a real place in Ghana, send them and we’ll consider them for its region page.

  • They must be yours. Only send photos you took yourself.
  • Say where. Tell us the place and the town, so we can file it under the right region.
  • You get the credit. We put your name (or the name you choose) under the photo and on our photo credits page.
  • What you agree to. Sending a photo means GhanaNice may show it on the site with your credit. You keep the copyright, and you can ask us to take it down at any time.
  • People in the picture. If someone can be clearly recognised, make sure they’re happy to be shown.
